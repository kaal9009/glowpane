Questions

What do I get?
MP4 video files (H.264, 30 fps, no sound). Many have a true black background, so only the decoration shows on the glass. You get a download link by email right after payment.
What will it play on?
A USB stick in a projector or TV, a laptop, a phone, a Fire Stick, or any media player that plays MP4.
Why four formats in the bundle?
Because real setups differ. Garage doors are much wider than a TV picture, rear projection flips text, and tall windows need a vertical picture.
Can I use it at my shop or event?
Yes. You may display the loops at your own home, business or event. You may not resell, share or re-upload the files.
How were these made?
Every loop is original animation, built in code by our small studio with AI-assisted tools. No film characters, no brand logos, no licensed music.
